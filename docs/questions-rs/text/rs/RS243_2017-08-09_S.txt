*241. DR. V . MAITREYAN: Will the
Minister of HOME AFFAIRS be pleased
to state:

financial and technological proposals for
the development of coastal security all
along the coasts of Tamil Nadu, if so, the
details thereof;

three years, year-wise and the equipments
including high speed steamer boats,
