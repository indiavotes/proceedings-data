Outcome of the BRICS Summit -
2014
*31. DR. V . MAITREYAN: Will the
Minister of EXTERNAL AFFAIRS be
pleased to state:
(a) whether the outcome of the BRICS
Summit - 2014 held at Brazil has brought
any positive gains to India in the fields of
trade and commerce, if so, the details
thereof;

and disagreements within BRICS member
countries in creating a BRICS Bank jointly
developed by BRICS countries;

are addressed by BRICS countries while
forming BRICS Bank; and
(d) the salient features of BRICS
Summit-2014 with respect to the BRICS
Bank and advantages and disadvantages for
India in creation of BRICS Bank?
