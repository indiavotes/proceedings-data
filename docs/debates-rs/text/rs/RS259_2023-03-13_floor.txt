OBITUARY REFERENCE  
  
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of 
Shri Narendra Pradhan, a former Member of this House, on 22 nd January, 2023.  He 
was a Member of this august House from July, 1992 to July, 1998. 
 Born on 7 th of April, 1947, at Gopalpur in Cuttack District of Odisha, Shri 
Pradhan was actively involved in organizing  youth camps to educate the rural youth 
for self-employment. He also took keen inte rest in providing legal aid to the poor and 
deprived sections of the society.   
 In the passing away of Shri Narend ra Pradhan, the country has lost a 
remarkably dedicated social worker an d a committed, able and disciplined 
parliamentarian. 
 We deeply mourn the passing away of Shri Narendra Pradhan. I request the 
hon. Members to rise in their places and ob serve silence as a mark of respect to the 
memory of the departed soul.    
 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute.) 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Secretary-General will conv ey to the members of the bereaved 
family our sense of profound sorrow and deep sympathy.
