n. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of 
a sitting Member of this house, Shri Hardwar Dubey and former Members, Shri Dawa 
Lama, Shrimati Usha Malhotra and Shri Solipeta Ramachandra Reddy. 
Shri Hardwar Dubey  passed away on the 26 th of June, 2023. He represented 
the State of Uttar Pradesh in  this august House. He was barely halfway through his 
term since its commencement in November, 2020. 
I share a personal bond with him. We bo th belong to the legal fraternity. Shri 
Dubey was a passionate conservationist at  heart, his deep commitment towards 
protection of wildlife and natural heritage was always reflected in his participation in 
the House. 
Born on 1
st June, 1949, at Husainabad in Ballia  District of Uttar Pradesh, Shri 
Dubey took special interest in  eradicating social problems  affecting the society. He 
served as a Member of Uttar Pradesh Legi slative Assembly for two terms and was 
also Minister of Finance in the Government of Uttar Pradesh. 
In the passing away of Shri Hardwar Dubey, the country has lost an able 
administrator, dedicated environmentalist and a committed parliamentarian. 
Shri Dawa Lama , a former Member of this House, who represented the State 
of West Bengal, from April 1996 to 2002, passed away on the 3rd of April this year. Shri 
Lama also served as a Member  of West Bengal Legislativ e Assembly and as Minister 
of State in the Government of West Bengal, holding various portfolios. 
Born on 18 th August, 1929, at Chong Tong Tea Estate in Darjeeling, West 
Bengal, Shri Lama worked assiduously for the upliftment of poor peasants and tea 
garden workers. 

 
In the passing away of Shri Dawa Lama, the country has lost a dedicated 
social worker, whose commitment to the cause of the upliftment of the most 
downtrodden was reflected equally both inside and outside this House. 
Shrimati Usha Malhotra  passed away on the 6 th of April, 2023. She 
represented the State of Himachal Pradesh in  this august House from April, 1980 to 
1986. 
Born on 6 th August, 1933, in Lahore, undivided  India, Shrimati Usha Malhotra 
was actively associated with the Indian Red Cross Society and women's welfare 
activities. An ardent advocate of women empowerment, she broke gender stereotype 
in public life.  She also represented the nation at various conferences in Geneva, 
Moscow and Brighton.  
 With the loss of Shrimati Usha Malh otra, the country has lost an ardent 
advocate of gender justice and a face of women empowerment.   
Shri Solipeta Ramachandra Reddy  who passed away on 27
th June, 2023 
represented the State of Andhra Pradesh fr om April 1996 to 2002.  Shri Reddy also 
served as a Member of the Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly from 1972 to 1978.   
Born on 17 th May, 1935 in Siddipet District of  the present Stat e of Telangana, 
Shri Reddy worked relentlessly for the cause of farmers, workers, women and always 
advocated for greater harmony among fellow citizens. 
In the passing away of Shri Solipeta Ramachandra Reddy, the country has lost 
a committed soul who represented the intere sts of the most downtrodden in this 
House. 
We deeply mourn the passing away of Shri Hardwar Dubey, Shri Dawa Lama, 
Shrimati Usha Malhotra and Shri Solipeta Ramachandra Reddy.
