Hon. Members, I extend Birthday Greet ings to hon. Members of Parliament, 
Dr. K. Laxman and Shri Harbhajan Singh. 
Dr. K. Laxman is Member of this augu st House since July, 2022. He has rich 
and varied experience in pub lic life. Having represented Musheerabad twice – first in 
the Eleventh Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly from 1999 to 2004 and later in the 
First Telangana Legislative Assembly from 2014 to 2018, he was the Floor Leader of 
the BJP from 2014 to 2016 in Telangana A ssembly. He is currently the National 
President of his party’s OBC Morcha. A doctorate in Geology from Osmania 
University, Dr. Laxman has always advo cated and championed the welfare of 
marginalized.   
He has a deep passion for sports, was a league-level cricketer and volleyball 
player. He is actively involved in Telangana and Andhra  Pradesh Olympic 
Associations. Married to Smt. Kova Uma, the couple is blessed with daughters, 
Shweta and Shruti and son Rahul. 
I join this House to wish Dr. K. Laxman  the best of health and happiness and 
extend greetings to his family members. 
Shri Harbhajan Singh, known for his off-spin in cricket, is a Member of this 
House since 2022.  He is amongst the top th ree off-spinners and regarded as one of 
the best bowlers in his area. 
Shri Harbhajan Singh, the Turbanator, play ed a pivotal role in India’s victory in 
T20 World Cup in 2007 and ODI World Cup in  2011. Just within three years of his 
debut, he claimed the historic test hat- trick against Australia in 2001. With his 103 
Test Matches, 236 One Day Internationals and several T20 matches, Shri Harbhajan 
Singh has left a truly inspirational legacy. He was honoured  with the Arjuna Award in 

2003 and conferred the Civilian Award, Padm a Shri, in 2009 by the Government of 
India.  Married to Smt. G eeta Basra, the couple is bl essed with daughter, Hinaya, 
and son Jovan.  I join this House to wish Shri Harbhajan Singh and his family the best 
of health and happiness and extend greetings to his family members.
