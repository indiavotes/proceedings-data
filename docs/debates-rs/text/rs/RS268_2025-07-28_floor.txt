Council of States (Rajya Sabha), submission of  physical notices are discontinued

Short Notice Question, Half-an-Hour Discussion and Notice for Question from Private 
Member and also notices for raising matters with the permission of the Chair (Zero 
Hour submission) through the Digital Sansad Portal only”.
