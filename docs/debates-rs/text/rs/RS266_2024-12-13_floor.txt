BIRTHDAY GREETINGS 
MR. CHAIRMAN:   Hon. Members, I am pleas ed to extend greetings to hon. Member 
of Parliament, Dr. Sarfraz Ahmad, and these are advanced greetings as his birthday is 
on Sunday, the 15 th of December. Dr. Ahmad, the fl oor leader of Jharkhand Mukti 
Morcha, is a Member of this House since May, 2024. Dr. Ahmad served as Reader at 
Bhagalpur University for 12 years before he rose to become the Education Minister in 
Bihar State Government. His extensive legislative experiences include as a Member of 
Bihar and Jharkhand Legislative Assemblies, Bihar Legislative Council and Member of 
the Eighth Lok Sabha, that is, 1984 to 1989.     
He is married to Shrimati Zainab Ahmed and the couple is blessed with a son, 
Saad Ahmed and a daughter, Zara Ahmed.  
On behalf of this House, I wish him a long, healthy and happy life and extend 
greetings to his family members and friends.

HE CHAIR 
23rd anniversary of the tragic terrorist attack on the Parliament Building 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, today marks the 23 rd anniversary of the tragic 
terrorist attack on the Parliament. 
On this fateful day in 2001, as Bharatiya Sansad , the sacred temple of our 
democracy, came under attack by those who sought to destabilize our nation, 
resolute bravery of our security personne l stood firm in thwarting their malicious, 
sinister plans. Hon. Members, let us take a moment to remember and honor the 
brave souls who lost their lives on that tragic day. 
This day should also serve  as a stark reminder of the need for vigilance and 
unity in the face of challenges to our nation al security. We must remain steadfast in 
our resolve to protect the sovereignty of our nation and ensure that the ideals of 
peace, liberty, justice and democracy prevail and blossom. 

I request hon. Members to rise in their places  and observe silence as a mark of 
respect to the memory of those who lost their lives in that terror attack. 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for a minute.)

milestone in Indian sporting history setting a global benchmark.

youngest World Chess Champion ever crowne d vanquishing Ding Liren of China in a

Gukesh's phenomenal triumph not only  emblazons our sporting legacy, but 
also epitomizes resurgent India where young titans emerge to dominate world stages. 
It strengthens our resolve to bid for hosting Olympic 2036. 
On behalf of this august House and our illustrious nation, I convey our 
profound acclaim to D. Gukesh. As our ma gnificent Tricolour as cends in Singapore

Indians.
