BIRTHDAY GREETINGS 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, I am pleased to extend greetings to hon'ble 
Members of Parliament, Shri Parimal Nathwa ni, Shri Ajit Kumar Bhuyan and Shri V. 
Vijayendra Prasad on their birthday today. 
 Shri Nathwani, an industrialist, is current ly in his third term and has been in the 
Rajya Sabha since June, 2008. An ardent Lo rd Krishna devotee, he is known for his 
humility and disarming modesty. He is also  the President of Gu jarat State Football 
Association. 
 Shri Parimal is married to Shrimati Varsha Nathwani and has a son, Dhanraj 
Nathwani.   
 Shri Ajit Kumar Bhuyan has been a Memb er of this august House since April, 
2020. He is a prolific Assa mese author who has written books focusing on Assam’s 
society and policies. His diverse career in cludes roles as a journalist, editor for 
prominent Assamese publications and involv ement in various social and human rights 
organizations. 
 Shri V. Vijayendra Prasad, an ackno wledged script writer and renowned 
Director, has been a Nominated Member of the Rajya Sabha since July, 2022. 
 The hon. Member has impactfully presen ted insights into various facets of the 
entertainment industry. He was married to late Shrimati Rajanandini and has a son, 
S.S. Rajamouli.   
 Hon. Members, on your and my own behalf, I wish them a long, healthy and 
happy life and extend greetings to their family members and friends.
