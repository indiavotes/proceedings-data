BIRTHDAY GREETINGS 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon Members, I am pleased to extend greetings to hon. Members 
of Parliament -- Shri Sharad Pawar and Shri Harsh Mahajan on their birthdays.  
Shri Sharad Pawar, Padma Vibhushan Aw ardee, a four-time Chief Minister of 
Maharashtra, -- 1978 to 1980, 1988 to 1990, 1990 to 1991 and 1993 to 1995 -- former 
Union Defence and Agriculture Minister, is serving his second term in this august 
House since April, 2020. Shri Pawar comes with extensive legislative experience of 
having served in Lok Sabha for seven term s, six terms in Maharashtra Legislative 
Assembly and one term in the Maharashtra Legislative Council. His long and 
distinguished career reflects his leadership  and deep influence in both State and 
national politics. His multifaceted persona lity and his administrative qualities have 
been witnessed while he had been the Pr esident of the ICC,  the BCCI and the 
Mumbai Cricket Association. His book, “On My Terms” , is a rich, insightful and 
remarkable memoir of his contributions to public life.   
Shri Sharad Pawar is married to Shrima ti Pratibha Pawar and the couple is 
blessed with daughter, Shrimati Supriya Sule, who was a former Member of this 
House, from 2006 to 2009 and, since then, cont inually she is in her fourth term in the 
Lok Sabha, having been recently elected in 2024.  
Hon. Members, on my own and on behalf  of this House, I wish him a happy, 
long and healthy life and extend greetings to  his family members. I cherish a strong 
personal bond with the family for the last over three decades.
