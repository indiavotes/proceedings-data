BIRTHDAY GREETINGS 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN:  Hon. Members, I take the o pportunity to wish birthday greetings to 
hon. Member of Parliament, Shri Jaggesh, who has been a Member of this august 
House since July, 2022.  He had also b een a Member of Karnataka Legislative 
Assembly (2008-09) and Legislative Council (2010-16). A devotee of Sri 
Raghavendra Swamy, Shri Jaggesh is also a popular Kannada actor. On my own and 
on your behalf, I wish him a long and a healthy life.   
 
 
OBITUARY REFERENCE  
  
MR. CHAIRMAN:  Hon. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of 
Shri Karnendu Bhattacharjee , a former Member of  t h i s  H o u s e ,  o n  2 3rd December, 
2022. He was a Member of this august Hous e for two consecutive terms, from April, 
1996 to 2008, from the State of Assam.  He had also served as a Member of Assam 
Legislative Assembly (1985 to 1990) and was a pioneer of co-operative movement in 
Assam. With his untimely death, the co untry has lost a doyen of cooperative 
movement and an able parliamentarian. We  deeply mourn the passing away of Shri 
Karnendu Bhattacharjee. I request the ho n. Members to rise in their places and 
observe silence as a mark of respect to the memory of the departed soul.    
 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute.) 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Secretary-General will conv ey to the members of the bereaved 
family our sense of profound sorrow and deep  sympathy. Now, Papers to be Laid on
