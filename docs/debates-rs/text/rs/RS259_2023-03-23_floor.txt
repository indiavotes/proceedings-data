 TRIBUTES TO MARTYRS BHAGAT SINGH, RAJGURU AND SUKHDEV 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN:  Hon. Members, today is the Ninety-Second Anniversary of 
Martyrdom of three greatest sons of Bhar at – Sardar Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and 
Sukhdev. These legendary heroes of our fr eedom struggle laid down their lives for 
India’s Independence, for our freedom, on this day in the year 1931.  Their supreme 
sacrifice at young age mark ed a decisive turnaround watershed moment in our 
freedom struggle. They chose the path of martyrdom, so that every Indian, including 
us, can live a life of freedom and dignity enjoying democratic values. Their legacy 
would ever be inspirational and motivation al for us and future generations. They 
exemplify the highest commitment to nationalism and freedom. 
 On this day, let us resolve to uphold  and cherish the valu es for which these 
martyrs sacrificed their lives.  Let us pledge that we always keep Bharat first. 
 I request the hon. Members to rise in  their places and observe silence as a 
mark of our humble respect to these brave martyrs. 
 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute)
