OBITUARY REFERENCE 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of 
former Member of this House, Shri Abdul Samad Siddiqui, on November 21st, 2022.   
 Shri Siddiqui was a Member of this House from April, 1988, to April, 1994. Born 
on 5 th of February, 1939, at Raichur in Karn ataka, Shri Siddiqui established several 
educational institutions in Raichur. In the passing away of Shri Siddiqui, the country 
has lost a prominent educationist, a good human being and an able parliamentarian.   
 We deeply mourn the passing away  of Shri Abdul Samad Siddiqui.  
 I request the Members to rise in their places and observe silence as a mark of 
respect to the memory of the departed.  
 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute.) 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Secretary-General will conv ey to the members of the bereaved 
family our sense of profound sorrow and deep sympathy.
