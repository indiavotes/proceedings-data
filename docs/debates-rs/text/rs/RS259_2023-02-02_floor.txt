OBITUARY REFERENCE 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN : I must reach out to the rear, backbenchers also. 
...(Interruptions)... 
Hon. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of former 
Union Law Minister, Shri Shanti Bhushan, on January 31, 2023.  He was a Member of 
this august House from July, 1977 to April, 1980. 
Born on 11th November, 1925, at Bijnore, in Uttar Pradesh, Shri Bhushan was a 
distinguished senior advocate and noted juri st who also served as Advocate General, 
Uttar Pradesh.  He was also Chairman of the Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh. 
I personally came to know him in 1989 upon being elected to the 9 th Lok Sabha 
and then shared a lasting personal relationship with him. 
In his death, nation has lost an outsta nding legal luminary, a man of standing 
qualities who always exemplified his conduct by sublimity and public interest. 
We will observe silence as a mark of respect. 
 
(Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute) 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN : Secretary-General will convey to  the members of the bereaved 
family our sense of profound sorrow and deep sympathy.
