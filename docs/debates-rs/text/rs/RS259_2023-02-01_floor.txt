OBITUARY REFERENCE 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, I refer with profound sorrow to the passing away of 
Dr. Ramkripal Sinha  on 15th of January, 2023. Born on 3rd January, 1936, Dr. Sinha 
served as a Member of this august House fr om 1974 to 1980 and as a Member in the 
Union Council of Ministers from 1977 to 1979 . His contributions as Cabinet Minister of 
Government of Bihar and as a Professor of  English in various colleges across States 
of West Bengal and Bihar are commendable. 
 In the passing away of Dr. Sinha, the country has lost an outstanding 
academician, committed Parliamentarian and an able administrator. We deeply mourn 
his untimely demise.  I request the hon. Members to rise in their places to observe 
silence as a mark of respect to the departed noble soul. 
 
 (Hon. Members then stood in silence for one minute.) 
 
MR. CHAIRMAN:  Secretary-General will co nvey to the member s of the bereaved 
family our sense of profound sorrow and deep sympathy.
