BIRTHDAY GREETINGS 
MR. CHAIRMAN : Hon. Members, I extend heartf elt birthday greetings to hon. 
Members of Parliament, Shri Jagat Prakash Nadda and Shri Tejveer Singh.  
Shri Jagat Prakash Nadda, polite, ever  cheerful, presently Leader of the 
House, is Minister of Health and Family Welfare and Chemicals and Fertilizers, as also 
National President of the Bh aratiya Janata Party. Nadda ji has served three terms as 
MLA and held key positions in Cabinet, in cluding Health, Parliamentary Affairs and 
Environment in Himachal Pradesh.  Shri Na dda, currently in his third term in Rajya 
Sabha, also served as Union Health Minister  from 2014 to 2019. His journey in public 
life took a firm shape during the Samp oorn Kranti Movement  led by Lok Nayak 
Jayaprakash Narayan in 1975. Embarking on his journey through student activism, he 
rose to become National Secretary of ABVP in 1989 and its President in 1991. 
Beyond public life, Shri Nadda ji excelled in sports, representing Bihar in 
swimming championships during his sch ool days. Dr. Mallika Nadda, his spouse, a 
distinguished Professor, is deeply committe d to advancing the welfare of specially-
abled children, championing th eir rights and opportunities for a better future. She is 
the President of Special Olympics Bharat and, in May, 2024 she was appointed as the 
Chairperson of Asia-Pacific Advisory Counc il of Special Olympics.  I was recently 
privileged to inaugurate Asia-Pacific Boc ce and Bowling Competition in Delhi, an 
event that was meticulously organised under her dynamic leadership, where 81 
specially-abled athletes from 12 countries participated. The event was spectacular 
and the attendance was absolutely amazing. Nadda ji's mother-in-law, Shrimati 
Jayashree Banerjee, is a former Lok Sabh a MP and a former Cabinet Minister in 
Madhya Pradesh, reflecting a legacy of public service he inherits. 
The couple is blessed with two sons, Harish, a lawyer, and Girish, an MBA graduate.  
On my own and on behalf of  all Members of the House,  I wish him a long, happy, 
healthy life in service of the nation.  
Shri Tejveer Singh is serving his first term as a Member of the Rajya Sabha 
since April 2024, representing the State of  Uttar Pradesh.  A prominent farmers' 

leader and a three-term Lok Sabha Member of Parliament from 1996 to 2004, Shri 
Tejveer had also served as Chairman of Uttar Pradesh Cooperative Bank, Lucknow. 
Tejveerji has always strived to work at th e grassroots, working for the welfare of the 
farming community. In fact, he is only the s econd Rajya Sabha MP from the district of 
Mathura. 
He is married to Shrimati Gyan Kumari  and the couple is blessed with three 
sons, Amit Chaudhary, Dr. Raghvendra  Chaudhary, Dr. Kunal Chaudhary and 
daughter Shrimati Meenakshi Chaudhary.  
On behalf of this House, I wish him a long, healthy and happy life and extend 
greetings to his family members.
