VALEDICTORY REMARKS 
MR. CHAIRMAN: Hon. Members, with the conclusion of the 270 th Session of the 
Rajya Sabha, we mark the end of the Budget Session of Parliament. I am deeply 
appreciative of your valuable perspectives that enriched the discussions in the House. 
Among the three Sessions of the Parliament, the Budget Session holds the place of 
primacy.  It is not only the longest of  all three Sessions but also the most 
consequential in shaping the nation’s grow th trajectory. The budgetary allocations 
approved, the policies endorsed and the priorities affirmed during this Session have a 
direct bearing on the life of every citizen of Bharat. 
[ 18 April, 2026 ]
5

 
 
Hon. Members, the Session commenced wi th the discussion on the Motion of 
Thanks to the hon.  President’s Address spanning over four days, during which 79 
Members of this House participated with effusive energy and earnestness. The reply 
of the hon. Prime Minister to the discussion  on the Motion of Thanks brought clarity 
and context to the many points raised by the Hon. Members across the aisle. 
The discussion on the Union Budget  2026–27 was equally incisive and 
comprehensive.  Spanning over four days, it witne ssed the participation of as many 
as 97 Members. Besides, the House also he ld substantial discussions on the working 
of two key Ministries of the Government .  The House also took note of the suo motu 
Statements made by the Minister of Comm erce and Industry on India-US Bilateral 
Trade Agreement and the Minister of External Affairs regarding the situation in West 
Asia. The Statement made by the hon. Pr ime Minister on the ongoing West Asia 
conflict and the challenges face d by India especially to m eet its energy requirements 
as a result of it, presented the evolving situation in a proper perspective and 
underlined the need for a collective resolve of the nation to face it.  
I am pleased to note that as many as 50 Private Members’ Bills were 
introduced during this Session.  I am also  delighted to share that on 94 occasions, 
hon. Members spoke in 12 regional language s included in the Eighth Schedule to the 
Constitution.  
Overall, the House functioned for a to tal of 157 hours and 40 minutes. The 
productivity of this Session stood at 109. 87 per cent. During the Session, we had the 
opportunity to raise 117 questions, 446 Zero Hour submissions and 207 Special 
Mentions.  
Hon. Members, this Session also witne ssed the re-election of Shri Harivansh 
as the Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha fo r the third time. The hon. Prime Minister, 
along with Leaders and Members across th e political spectrum, felicitated Shri 
Harivansh on his re-election.  
I appreciate the cooperation extended by all in transacting the Business of the 
House and extend my appreciation to the hon. Deputy Chairman, Shri Harivansh  and 
Members on the Panel of Vice-Chairperson s for assisting me in conducting the 
proceedings of the House.  I also thank th e Leader of the House,  the Leader of the 
Opposition, the Minister of Parliamentary Affairs, the Leaders of various Political 
Parties and Groups and all the hon. Members for their kind cooperation extended by 
them.

I acknowledge the untiring efforts of the Secretary-General and his team of 
dedicated officers and staff in ensuring that the Session runs smoothly. I also thank 
the print and electronic media for covering the proceedings of the House and keeping 
the country informed.

has already been given.  Please do not raise it and waste the time of the House.  
When it is really required, by  taking the consent of the entire House, we will go for it.
