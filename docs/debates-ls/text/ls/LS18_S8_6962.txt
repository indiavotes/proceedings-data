Need to address the situation arising out of stray dog menace in Kerala-Laid

DR. M. P. ABDUSSAMAD SAMADANI (PONNANI): I wish to draw the attention of the Government towards a critical public health crisis in Kerala caused by the stray dog menace. In 2025 alone, over 3.69 lakh dog bite incidents were reported across the state, alongside 33 tragic rabies deaths?a five-year high. So far in 2026, dozens more lives have been lost. School students, daily wage labourers, and the homeless remain the most vulnerable, forced to navigate public roads and spaces without safety. Stray dogs have become a grave threat to public safety and public health. While the Supreme Court?s landmark May 19, 2026 judgement mandates the complete removal and institutional sheltering of stray dogs from high-footfall public zones like schools, hospitals, and bus depots, local bodies in Kerala struggle with execution due to acute infrastructural and funding deficits. Crucially, the apex court affirmed that the right to human life takes precedence and permitted strict euthanasia for rabid, incurably ill, or demonstrably aggressive dogs under statutory protocols. I urge the Central Government to collaborate with the state to strengthen Animal Birth Control infrastructure and ensure the strict implementation of the Supreme Court's directions?enforcing mandatory relocation from public premises and executing lawful euthanasia for dangerous strays to protect our citizens.
