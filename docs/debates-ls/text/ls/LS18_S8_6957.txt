Need for construction of a new rail line from Jaynagar to Maipith via Jamtala at Sealdah-Jaynagar section in South 24 Parganas, West Bengal-Laid

SHRIMATI PRATIMA MONDAL (JAYNAGAR): I would like to draw the attention of the hon?ble Minister of Railways regarding a long pending demand which is construction of a new railway line from Jaynagar to Maipith via Jamtala at Sealdah-Jaynagar section in the South 24 Parganas district. I have raised this issue in the august House many times and also discussed the importance of this rail line project in the Eastern railway and in review meeting as well. I would also like to remind the hon?ble Minister of Railways about his visit to Kultali during 2026 assembly election campaign where he assured the construction of new rail line from Jaynagar to Maipith for the convenience and well being of the local people. I therefore request the hon?ble Railway Minister once again to take necessary steps at the earliest to fulfill the promise made to the people of Kultali for construction of Jaynagar to Maipith via Jamtala rail line project.
