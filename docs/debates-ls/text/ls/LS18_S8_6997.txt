Need to introduce Vande Bharat Express between Nagpur and Mumbai with stoppage at Akola Junction.-laid

SHRI ANUP SANJAY DHOTRE (AKOLA): I would like to draw the attention of the Government to the urgent need for introducing a new Vande Bharat Express between Nagpur and Mumbai with a scheduled halt at Akola Junction. Sir, the recently introduced Nagpur?Pune Vande Bharat Express has received an encouraging response from passengers, and the Railways is reportedly considering augmenting its capacity by adding more coaches. This clearly reflects the growing demand for fast, comfortable, and efficient rail connectivity from the Vidarbha region. In this context, the introduction of a Nagpur?Mumbai Vande Bharat Express with a halt at Akola Junction would greatly benefit thousands of passengers, including IT professionals, students, patients seeking advanced medical treatment, businesspersons, government employees, and workers who regularly travel between Vidarbha and Mumbai. Akola serves as a major railway junction and caters to a large population from the surrounding districts. I, therefore, urge the Government to kindly consider the introduction of a Nagpur?Mumbai Vande Bharat Express with a scheduled halt at Akola Junction in the larger public interest.
