Regarding likely problems due to proposed emergency landing facility on National Highway in Singarayakonda, Prakasam district, Andhra Pradesh-Laid

SHRI MAGUNTA SREENIVASULU REDDY (ONGOLE): I wish to draw the attention of the Government towards a matter of urgent public importance regarding the proposed Emergency Landing Facility on the National Highway in Singarayakonda Mandal of Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh. The proposed project affects Mulaguntapadu, Kalikivayi, Kanumalla and several neighbouring villages. The local residents have expressed serious apprehensions regarding its likely impact on habitations, agricultural lands, livelihoods, local road connectivity and day-to-day movement. They have also represented that no adequate consultation has been held with the affected stakeholders before proceeding with the project. I had earlier brought these concerns to the notice of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways and also Chairman, NHAI, requesting that the grievances of the affected people be examined sympathetically before any further action is taken and asks for the clarification regarding the ?No Obstruction Zone?. However, it has now been learnt that NHAI has floated the tender for the project, causing widespread concern among the local population. While national infrastructure development and aviation safety are important, it should be with legitimate concerns of affected communities. I, therefore, urge the Government to keep the tender process in abeyance, undertake comprehensive public consultation with the affected villagers and stakeholders and proceed further only after addressing their genuine concerns.
