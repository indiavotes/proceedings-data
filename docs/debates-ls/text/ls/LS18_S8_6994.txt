Regarding steps undertaken to promote green cities and towns aligning with Sustainable Development Goals.-laid

DR. RABINDRA NARAYAN BEHERA (JAJPUR): I would like to draw the attention of Minister of Environment, Forest and Climate Change about the current status of forest and tree cover in the country and also provide me the status as to whether there has been any increase in green cover since 2014.
