Need to notify Ecological Sensitive Areas as per the recommendations made in Oommen V Oommen Report.-laid

ADV. DEAN KURIAKOSE (IDUKKI): The Kasturi Rangan-led High Level Working Group (HLWG) had recommended an area of 13,108.7 sq km in 123 villages in the state to be demarcated ecologically sensitive. However, the State Government, after physical verification, excluded an area of 3,114.30 sq km due to agriculture and settlements. The Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change accepted the proposal and the draft notification was published with an area of 9,993.70 sq km, inclusive of 123 villages. Later, the previous State Government further pointed out that the area under the draft notification constitutes two categories -- forest land constituting 9,107 sq km and a non-forest area of 886.7 sq km. The state proposed to remove the non-forest area of 886.7 sq km from Ecological Sensitive Areas (ESA) and sought to denote only 9,107 sq km as ESA with 118 villages. However, the proposal has not been cleared yet. In this regard, I request the Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change to notify ESAs in line with the Oommen V Oommen report.
