Need to set up a National Institute for Coffee Research and Climate Resilience in Kodagu, Karnataka.-laid

SHRI YADUVEER WADIYAR (MYSORE): Kodagu, the heart of India?s coffee industry, contributes significantly to the nation?s coffee production and exports while also sustaining the livelihoods of thousands of farmers and plantation workers. However, coffee cultivation today faces unprecedented challenges due to climate change, erratic rainfall, rising temperatures, emerging pests and diseases, and increasing production costs. These challenges threaten not only the coffee economy of Kodagu but also the ecological balance of the Western Ghats. I urge the Government of India to establish a National Institute for Coffee Research and Climate Resilience in Kodagu as a Centre of Excellence dedicated to coffee, spices, and sustainable plantation agriculture. The institute should work in collaboration with the Coffee Board of India, agricultural universities, ICAR institutions, and international research organizations to develop climate-resilient crop varieties, promote precision agriculture, strengthen pest and disease management, and support value addition and farmer training. Such an institution would benefit coffee-growing regions across India, enhance global competitiveness, promote sustainable agricultural practices, generate skilled employment, and reinforce India?s position as one of the world?s leading producers of high-quality coffee. I therefore request the Government to consider this proposal on priority.
