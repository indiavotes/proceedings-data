Need to establish an Integrated Fruit Pulp Research Centre with cold storage facility at Anaicut in Vellore, Tamil Nadu.-laid

SHRI D. M. KATHIR ANAND (VELLORE): The Anaicut Assembly Segment in Vellore Parliamentary Constituency is endowed with favourable agro-climatic conditions and fertile soils, making it an important horticultural region of Tamil Nadu. The area is well known for the cultivation of mango, banana, guava, custard apple and several other fruits, providing livelihood to a large number of farmers. Despite the region's immense horticultural potential, there is no Central Government-funded Integrated Fruit Pulp Research Centre or Integrated Cold Storage Facility to support scientific research, value addition, post-harvest management and marketing of fruit produce. Consequently, farmers suffer considerable post-harvest losses, inadequate processing facilities and reduced income. An Integrated Fruit Pulp Research Centre in Anaicut should undertake research on advanced fruit processing technologies, enhancement of shelf life, development of value-added products, phytochemical analysis of regional fruits for nutraceutical and pharmaceutical applications, and innovative green technologies using fruit-based bioactive compounds. The Centre should also include an Integrated Cold Storage Facility with pre-cooling, grading, sorting, ripening, packaging and scientific storage infrastructure to minimise wastage, improve quality, facilitate exports and ensure better price realisation for farmers. The establishment of such a Centre would strengthen horticultural research, promote food processing industries, generate rural employment, encourage agri-entrepreneurship and substantially enhance the income of farmers across Vellore and neighbouring districts. I, therefore, urge the Union Government to allocate adequate funds and take expeditious steps to establish an Integrated Fruit Pulp Research Centre with an Integrated Cold Storage Facility in Anaicut Assembly Segment of Vellore Parliamentary Constituency, Tamil Nadu.
